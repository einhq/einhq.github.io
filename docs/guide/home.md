# Home, memories and statistics

Home is the page EiN opens on: pictures taken on this day in earlier years, the albums people made
lately, and the birthdays and anniversaries around today. The **Statistics** page shows how the
archive has grown and when in the year births, weddings and deaths fall. This page is for everyone
who uses EiN.

## Open Home

Choose **Home** at the top of the sidebar. On a computer, the EiN mark at the top left also takes
you there. EiN opens on Home when you sign in, unless you were on your way to another page.

The page is headed **Welcome**, "What is new in the archive, what is coming up, and this day in
years past". Under the header:

- **On this day** runs across the top, when there is anything to show;
- **News** fills the wider column on the left;
- **Events** stands in a card on the right. On a narrow window or a phone it comes after News, so
  scroll down past the news to see it.

![The Home page of the Wrenfield family's archive: the Welcome header, News with four albums the Administrator added 2 weeks ago, each with a row of pictures ending in a +N tile, and an Events card with one entry, Noah Price turns 7, in 4 weeks](../../assets/shots/home.webp "News lists the albums made lately and Events what comes round in the next month. On this day is missing here because nothing was taken on that date in earlier years.")

Each section loads on its own, so one can appear before the others. All three show only what you
may see: an album or a person that is not shared with you does not appear. "Today" is the date on
your own computer or phone, not the server's.

What fills Home comes from the modules installed. The Archive module fills all three sections;
without it, Home has nothing to show.

## On this day

**On this day** is a row of round pictures, one for each year that has pictures taken on today's
date, newest year first, up to 8. Under each ring it says how long ago that was, such as "1 year
ago" or "12 years ago", and the year. The picture in the ring is the first one taken that day.
This year counts too, so a picture taken today has a ring of its own, "0 years ago".

When there are more rings than fit, swipe the row sideways, or scroll it with a trackpad.

The row counts pictures in albums only. Pictures still in your staging area, and pictures with no
date, are not part of it, and neither are pictures you may not see. When no year has anything for
today, the section is not shown at all.

The same rings stand above the feed on the **Media** page; see
[Media, Year and labels](media.md#browse-the-feed).

### Watch the memories story

1. Press a ring. The story opens over the whole window, on black.
2. At the top, a thin bar for each picture of that year fills as the picture shows; each picture
   stays 5 seconds. Under the bars it says how long ago it was, such as "7 years ago", and the
   date, such as "25 December 2019".
3. The story plays that year's pictures in the order they were taken, up to 24 of them, then moves
   on to the next earlier year with pictures from this day, through up to 30 years that have
   pictures. After the last picture of the oldest year, it closes.

| To                             | With a finger or the mouse                                                                          | With the keyboard |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Go to the next picture         | Tap or click anywhere but the left third                                                            | <kbd>→</kbd>      |
| Go back a picture              | Tap or click the left third                                                                         | <kbd>←</kbd>      |
| Pause, and play again          | Hold your finger or the mouse button down, and let go to play on; or press **Pause**, then **Play** | <kbd>Space</kbd>  |
| See the pictures in the viewer | **Open in the lightbox**                                                                            |                   |
| Close the story                | **Close**                                                                                           | <kbd>Esc</kbd>    |

**Open in the lightbox** opens the story's pictures, from every year, in the viewer at the picture
you were on, with the details panel and everything else the viewer offers; see
[Viewing photos, videos and recordings](viewer.md).

A few things work differently from a slideshow in the viewer:

- The 5 seconds are fixed. Your **Seconds per slide** preference applies to the viewer's slideshow
  only; see [Watch a slideshow](viewer.md#watch-a-slideshow).
- Each picture pans and zooms slowly whatever your **Ken Burns effect** preference says. On a device
  set to reduce motion, the pictures stay still.
- A video shows as a still from it, and a recording as an empty slide. Use **Open in the lightbox**
  to play them.
- While a picture is still loading, its bar waits, so a slow picture still gets its full time.

While the story plays, your screen is kept from going to sleep.

## News

**News** is a timeline of the albums people made lately, newest first, up to 8. Each entry reads
"&lt;who&gt; added &lt;album&gt;", for example "Helen Okafor added Christmas in Wellington", with
how long ago under it:

| It says                       | When the album was made                   |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| "just now"                    | less than an hour ago                     |
| "3h ago"                      | less than a day ago, in hours             |
| "yesterday"                   | one day ago                               |
| "4 days ago"                  | up to a week ago                          |
| "2 weeks ago"                 | up to a month ago                         |
| a date, such as "12 Jul 2025" | longer ago, in your browser's date format |

Under it is a row of the album's pictures, as many as fit and at most five, the last one marked
with how many more the album holds, such as **+21**. Click the album's name or any of the pictures
to open the album.

Beside each entry is the portrait of the person whose account made the album, when the account is
linked to a person in the archive; otherwise a picture icon. See
[Your profile, favourites and trash](profile.md).

What makes an entry:

- **An album is made.** Every way of making one counts: **Create → Album**, **New album** while
  adding pictures, and filing a group from staging as a **New album** or **New private album**. See
  [Create an album](albums.md#create-an-album) and [Sorting in staging](staging.md).
- **The time is when the album was made.** Adding pictures to an album, renaming it or changing
  its dates does not bring it back to the top.
- **Only albums in the album list count**: folders inside an album, and the albums staging suggests
  to you, do not. An album moved to the trash leaves the news.

When nothing has been added yet, News says "Nothing has been added yet" and "What people add shows
up here.", and when you are signed in it offers **Import media**; see
[Import media](importing.md#import-media).

## Events

**Events** is a card of what comes round every year, for the days around today: the number of
entries beside its title, then up to three groups.

- **Today**
- **Coming up**, nearest first
- **Recently**, the most recent first

At the right of each entry it says when: "Today", "Tomorrow", "Yesterday", "in 9 days" or "3 days
ago" up to 13 days away, and after that in weeks, "in 3 weeks" or "2 weeks ago". Each entry shows
the face of the person it is about, or both partners' faces for a wedding anniversary. Click it to
open the person's page.

The card looks 7 days back and 30 days ahead, and holds up to 16 entries. When there are more, the
milestones are kept first, then the ones nearest to today. When nothing falls in that time, it
says "Nothing comes round in the next few weeks."

### What appears in Events

| Kind                | Comes from                                  | When it shows                                                                                                                  | It reads                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Birthday            | A person's **Born** date                    | Every year, up to the age of 110                                                                                               | "Helen Okafor turns 68"; after the person's death, "Arthur Wrenfield would have turned 95"                                                             |
| Memorial            | A person's **Died** date                    | Only in milestone years                                                                                                        | "5 years since Peggy Wrenfield died"                                                                                                                   |
| Wedding anniversary | The **From** date of two partners' relation | Every year, up to and including the year the relation ended (**Until**) or the first of the two died, and for at most 75 years | "Jon Wrenfield and Kiri Wrenfield — 30 years married"; "… years together" for **Partner**, "… years engaged" for an engagement the importer brought in |

Each of them needs a full date, with year, month and day. A date known only to the year or the
month, such as `1934` or `1934-05`, has no day to come round on, so it makes no entry. Someone born
on 29 February has a birthday entry in leap years only. The first entry is the first anniversary:
nobody "turns 0".

Birthdays and memorials are read from the people on the **People** page. A **Marriage** event on a
person's page does not make an anniversary; the **From** date on the partner relation does.

### Milestones

A milestone is the 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th year, and then every 10th: 30, 40, 50 and so on. A
milestone shows in bold, and milestones are the entries kept when the card runs out of room.
Memorials appear only on milestones; birthdays and wedding anniversaries appear every year.

### An example

In the Wrenfield family's archive, the Events card on 20 January 2026 shows four entries, the
milestones in bold:

| Group     | Entry                                                   | When       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Coming up | **Grace Price turns 40**                                | in 5 days  |
|           | **Jon Wrenfield and Kiri Wrenfield — 30 years married** | in 2 weeks |
|           | Thomas Wrenfield turns 65                               | in 4 weeks |
| Recently  | **5 years since Peggy Wrenfield died**                  | 3 days ago |

### Make someone's dates appear

- **Birthdays and memorials:** open the person, press **Edit**, and type **Born** or **Died** as a
  full date, such as `1958-05-03`. See [Edit a person](people.md#edit-a-person).
- **Wedding anniversaries:** give the partner relation a **From** date, such as `1996-02-03`, when
  you choose **Add partner** on the person's **Family** tab, and an **Until** date if it ended for
  another reason than a death. Choose **Married** under **Relationship** for "years married". In
  the web interface this takes an administrator. To date a partnership that is already recorded,
  choose **Add partner** again and pick the same partner. See
  [Partnerships and their dates](family.md#partnerships-and-their-dates).

## The Statistics page

Choose **Statistics** in the sidebar, under **System**. Everyone can open it. The page is headed
**Statistics**, "Archive statistics over time", and holds two sections: **Events** and **Nodes**.

![The Statistics page: the Events section with four charts, Births by month with a total of 19, Engagements by month with 0, Marriages by month with 10 and Deaths by month with 2, each with a bar for every month from Jan to Dec, and the Nodes heading below them](../../assets/shots/stats.webp "Each chart folds every year into twelve months, so you see when in the year births, weddings and deaths fall.")

### When in the year life events fall

The **Events** section, from the Archive module, has four charts: **Births by month**,
**Engagements by month**, **Marriages by month** and **Deaths by month**.

- Each has a bar for every month from Jan to Dec, counting all years together, as the line "All
  years" under it says. Rest the pointer on a bar to read its count, such as "Mar: 2".
- The number at the top right is the total. It also counts dates known only to the year, which are
  in no month's bar, so the bars can add up to less than the total.
- **Births** and **Deaths** count the **Born** and **Died** dates of people.
- **Engagements** and **Marriages** count the **Engagement** and **Marriage** events written on
  people's pages. A wedding written on both spouses counts twice, and a partnership dated only by a
  **From** date on the relation does not count. See [Record a wedding](family.md#record-a-wedding).

### How the archive grew

The **Nodes** section shows what has been added to EiN, year by year. Everything in EiN is a node:
each photo, album, person and place, and each file EiN makes from them; see
[How EiN works](../start/how-ein-works.md).

- **File storage growth** runs across the full width: the size of the files added each year,
  counting the originals and the smaller versions EiN makes of them, with a line for "Cumulative
  file size by month". The total is at the top right.
- Then one chart for each kind of node, such as **Person nodes** or **Album nodes**, **File nodes**
  first and the others largest first:
  bars for the nodes created in each calendar year, a line for the "Cumulative total by month", and
  the total, such as "2,431 nodes". Rest the pointer on a bar to read the year's count.

A node counts in the year it was created in EiN. Material brought in from an older archive by the
importer keeps the dates it had there. The charts leave out the kinds that make up the system
itself, such as folders, links, accounts and sessions, and they count what you may see.

These charts can take a few seconds to appear on a large archive. When there is nothing to count,
the section says "No node history found".

## When something goes differently

| What you see                                                       | Why, and what to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **On this day** is missing                                         | No picture in the albums you may see was taken on today's date in another year. Pictures still in staging and pictures without a date do not count; file them into an album and check their dates, see [Dates and places of pictures](dates-places.md).                                   |
| A picture turns up under the day before or after                   | The day is counted in UTC, so a picture taken close to midnight can fall on the neighbouring day. A picture known only to a year or a month counts from the start of that period, so it can turn up on 1 January or the first of the month, or the day before in a time zone east of UTC. |
| The story says "Nothing was taken on this day in earlier years."   | There are no pictures you may see from that date, for example when the story was opened from a link for another day.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| An album you added pictures to is not in News                      | News shows when an album was made, not when pictures were added to it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| An entry reads "Somebody added …"                                  | EiN could not tell which account made the album, for example because that account is gone.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A birthday or memorial is missing                                  | The **Born** or **Died** date needs a day, the person must be on the **People** page, and birthdays stop at 110 years. Memorials show only in milestone years. The card also holds at most 16 entries, milestones first.                                                                  |
| A wedding anniversary is missing                                   | The partner relation needs a **From** date with a day. Anniversaries stop after the relation's **Until** year, after the year one of the two died, and after 75 years. A **Marriage** event alone does not make one.                                                                      |
| Events is a day out, or **On this day** shows yesterday's pictures | Home takes today's date from your computer or phone. Check its date and time zone.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A red box above the sections                                       | One section could not be loaded, and the box gives the reason. The other sections still show. Reload the page; if the box stays, tell whoever looks after EiN.                                                                                                                            |
| The **Marriages** chart shows twice as many weddings as there were | Each wedding was written as an event on both spouses, and each event counts. See [When in the year life events fall](#when-in-the-year-life-events-fall).                                                                                                                                 |

## In the terminal

Home and the Statistics page run ordinary commands, which you can run yourself in
[the terminal](../shell/terminal.md). The Home page passes your own date to them; run in the
terminal, they take today's date in UTC unless you name a day.

```bash
# in EiN's terminal
onthisday                                  # what On this day shows, one line a year
onthisday 12-25 --limit 20                 # another day, more years
news                                       # what News shows, with the time in UTC
news --limit 20
events                                     # what Events shows: 7 days back, 30 days ahead
events --today 2026-01-20                  # around another date
events --days-ahead 0 --days-past 0        # today only
timeline --kind birth,death,anniversary    # the family's history, looking back from today
dayinhistory 12-25 --years 20 --per-year 1 # the pictures of a day, by path
person-stats                               # the counts behind the Statistics page's Events charts
```

`events --today 2026-01-20` in the Wrenfield family's archive prints the example above. A `*` marks
a milestone:

```text
2026-01-17    3d ago  * 5 years since Peggy Wrenfield died
2026-01-25     in 5d  * Grace Price turns 40
2026-02-03    in 14d  * Jon Wrenfield and Kiri Wrenfield — 30 years married
2026-02-19    in 30d    Thomas Wrenfield turns 65
```

`onthisday` prints one line for each year, such as `2019  Pictures taken on 25 December 2019`, and
`news` one line for each album, such as `2026-10-11 18:02  Helen Okafor added Christmas in
Wellington`. Each of the three gathers what every installed module offers; with the Archive module
those are `onthisday-media`, `news-albums` and `events-anniversaries`.

`timeline` is the archive's history, newest first: albums, births, deaths, birthdays, wedding
anniversaries, events, moves and GPS tracks. It looks back from today, never ahead, and stops a
person's birthdays at their death. By default it lists only milestone birthdays and anniversaries;
`--birthdays all` and `--anniversaries all` list every year. `--kind`, `--year`, `--person`,
`--location` and `--tag` narrow it.

The full options are in the reference for [`onthisday`](../reference/commands-core.md#onthisday),
[`news`](../reference/commands-core.md#news), [`events`](../reference/commands-core.md#events),
[`timeline`](../reference/commands-archive.md#timeline),
[`dayinhistory`](../reference/commands-archive.md#dayinhistory) and
[`person-stats`](../reference/commands-archive.md#person-stats), and
[Commands by task](../shell/commands.md) lists more.
