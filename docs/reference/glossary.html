Glossary

This page defines the words the EiN documentation uses, in alphabetical order, for anyone who meets one and wants to know what it means. Each entry is short and links the page that explains the topic in full.

A few words mean two things in EiN, depending on where you meet them: an event is a change to the tree or a dated note on a person, a group is a set of accounts or a suggested album in staging, and a link is a softlink, a hard link or a share link. Each meaning has an entry of its own.

A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · I · J · L · M · N · O · P · Q · R · S · T · U · V · W

A

Access list

Further users and groups that a node grants access to, beyond its owner and group, each with its own read, write and execute, such as group:family:rwx. It is optional. The Share dialog fills it in for you; acl and share-set set it in the terminal. See Permissions in depth.

Account

A person who can sign in, with a username, a password and a home folder. Each account is a user node under /etc/users, belongs to groups and has a personal group named like itself. EiN starts with two accounts, admin and guest. See Accounts and groups.

Admin mode

A sign-in of a member of the sudo group that acts as the administrator for a while. It starts with Administer… in the account menu, or admin in a terminal, and ends with Leave admin mode, or admin --off. See Finding your way around.

Administrator

The admin account, uid 0, which passes every permission check, as root does on Linux. A member of the sudo group in admin mode passes them too. Some commands and pages, such as Nodes and Dashboard, are for administrators only. See Permissions in depth.

Album

A node of type album that gathers the photos, videos and recordings of one occasion. It holds the files uploaded into it and links to pictures added from elsewhere, and keeps its cover, its time span and its stacks. Albums are under /archive/albums. See Albums.

Archive

The first module, archive, and what it adds: albums, people and their family tree, locations, devices and their tracks, labels, staging, and the pipelines that process media. Its part of the tree is /archive. Without it, EiN keeps what you give it and works nothing out. See Modules.

Assistant

The chat that answers questions about what the archive holds and about EiN, by running EiN's commands as you. A command that would change something waits until you press Run. It comes with the assistant module and needs a language model. See Chatting with the assistant.

Attribute

A named value a node carries besides its name and permissions: a photo's size, checksum and capture time, a person's names and dates. The node's type decides which attributes it has. attrs shows them in the terminal. See Nodes and node types.

Audit trail

A record of every write to the tree: who created, changed, moved, linked or removed which node, when, and what changed. The audit command reads it, for administrators only. Writes under /var and terminal sessions leave no record. See Commands.

B

Bulk lane

The lowest place in the order of processing work. Work on a staging category of the Google Photos export or Scans kind runs there, so what people upload in the meantime is processed first. See Processing and pipelines.

Burst

Several shots taken a moment apart. A burst is not a duplicate: EiN folds it into a stack and removes nothing. See Duplicates.

C

Category

A folder of your staging area that keeps one source together, such as a Google Photos export, a box of scanned prints or a phone. Its kind (Inbox, Google Photos export, Scans, Phone, Camera, Messaging, Screenshots or Other) decides how its files are grouped, dated and ordered for processing. See Sorting in staging.

Container

One part of an installation, run by podman or Docker with compose: ein (EiN itself), db (PostgreSQL), and if you chose them ems (the media service) and llm (the language model). Only ein has ports open to other machines. A container is not a service. See How EiN works.

Core

EiN without modules: the tree, the shell and its commands, the pipeline engine, accounts and sharing, and the frame of the web interface. Core brings no pipelines of its own. See How EiN works.

Cover

The picture that stands for an album, a person, a location or a device on its card and in its header. A cover is a softlink to one of the pictures. See Albums and People and faces.

D

Dashboard

The administrator's page that sums up the system's state in one verdict (Healthy, Catching up, Stuck or Needs a person) with tabs for the checks, the work waiting, the media service, the services and the log. See Health and the dashboard.

Data folder

The folder on the machine's disk that holds your files: the originals in files, the renditions EiN makes from them in files-volatile. It is ~/ein/data unless you chose another at install. The database is kept apart, in the container engine's volume ein_db. See Files and storage.

Details panel

The panel beside a photo, video or recording in the viewer, with its date, place, people, labels, camera and the text read from it. i opens and closes it. See Viewing photos, videos and recordings.

Device

A camera, phone or tracker, matched by its make, model and serial number. A registered device dates the pictures it took in its own time zone and can keep a GPS track. Devices are under /archive/devices. See Devices and GPS tracks.

Discard

The staging decision that sends a group's files to your trash instead of filing them. It can be undone. See Sorting in staging.

Duplicate

A file that is the same picture as another. An exact copy is the same bytes and is kept once; a look-alike is the same picture in other bytes, marked with how alike the two are, for you to decide. A burst is not a duplicate. See Duplicates.

E

Embedding

A list of numbers that stands for a picture, a face, a voice or a text, so that similar things have similar numbers. Search by meaning, finding faces of the same person and searching with a picture all compare embeddings. The media service makes them. Also called a vector. See Search and the finder.

EMS

The media service. The name is short for EiN Media Service.

Event, a change

The announcement that a node was created, changed, moved or deleted, made once the change is saved. Pipelines start from events. See Changes and pipelines.

Event, on a timeline

A dated note on a person, an album, a location or a device: a wedding, a move, a birth. A person's event has the type Generic, Birth, Death, Marriage or Engagement. See Family tree, events and homes.

EXIF

The metadata a camera or phone writes into a picture: the camera, the time it was taken, the exposure and often where. EiN reads it on arrival and keeps it as the file's child metadata/exif. See Dates and places of pictures.

F

Favourite

A picture you have marked with a heart. A favourite is a softlink in the folder stars in your home folder, so hearting a picture never copies or moves it. See Your profile, favourites and trash.

File

A node of type file: stored bytes with a name, a mimetype, a size and a checksum. A photo, a video, a recording and a document are files. A media file keeps what EiN works out about it as children: metadata/, thumbnails/, regions/. See Files and storage.

Filing

The staging decision that moves a group's files into the library, as a new album, a new private album or into an album that exists. Only filing makes your uploads visible to anyone else. See Sorting in staging.

Finder

The box in the middle of the top bar, Search for anything. It finds pages, albums, people and places as you type, and Ctrl+K puts the cursor in it from anywhere. See Search and the finder.

Fingerprint

A short summary of a file's picture or sound that finds copies in other bytes, such as a smaller version or a re-encoded video. EiN makes one once a file is prepared and compares it to find look-alikes. See Duplicates.

Front

The photo a stack shows as its tile in an album's grid. Make front in the viewer chooses another. See Albums.

G

Group, of accounts

A set of accounts, kept under /etc/groups. Every node has one group, and an access list can name more. EiN starts with the groups admin, users, ssh, sudo and guest, and every account has a personal group. See Accounts and groups.

Group, in staging

A suggested album: files in a staging category that the organizer put together because they belong to one occasion, folder or year. You file, discard or put aside each group. See Sorting in staging.

Guest

Whoever opens EiN without signing in. The guest can sign in or open a share link and nothing more, unless an administrator turns on Visitors may browse; then the guest sees what is granted to the guest group. The guest never changes anything. See Permissions in depth.

H

Hard link

The same node under a second name in another folder, as on Unix. Unlike a softlink, it is not a node of its own. See Links.

Health

What the health command and the Dashboard report: a verdict, Healthy, Catching up, Stuck or Needs a person, and the checks behind it. See Health and the dashboard.

Home folder

Your own space in the tree, /home/<username>: your settings, shell history, favourites in stars, your trash and, with the Archive, your staging area. See The tree.

I

Importer

The importer module, which moves one particular older archive into EiN. It is not published and is not a general importer: photos from anywhere else come in through staging. See Getting your photos in.

Inbox

The staging category every account has, which cannot be removed. Uploads land in it unless you choose another category. See Sorting in staging.

Instance

One run of a pipeline on one node, with its steps and their status. A pipeline runs at most once per node at a time. The Work tab of the dashboard lists instances, and pipeline-status prints one. See Processing and pipelines.

J

Journal

The record of every staging decision, in staging/journal in your home folder, each with the plan that undoes it. The Done tab and staging undo undo an entry. See Staging and importing for administrators.

L

Label

One word, such as sailing, put on a picture, an album, a person, a location or a device. The same word is the same label across the archive. A label is a folder under /archive/tags holding a link to everything that carries it. See Media, Year and labels.

Lane

A part of a staging category's stream of groups: To sort, Everyday, Needs a date, Clutter and Later. Not to be confused with the bulk lane of processing. See Sorting in staging.

Language model

The model the assistant asks, either in the llm container on your machine or at a hosted provider with an OpenAI-compatible API. Everything except the chat works without one. See The language model.

Location

A named place, such as "Highbury", with an address, a description, labels, the people who lived there, and a point, a circle or a polygon on the map. Pictures whose position falls inside a circle or polygon are linked to it. Locations are under /archive/locations. See Locations and the map.

Log

A node that keeps lines appended to it for a time, such as the system log /var/logs/system. See Services and logs.

Look-alike

A file that shows the same picture as another in other bytes: a smaller version, a re-save in another format, a mild crop. EiN marks it with how alike the two are and suggests which to keep. See Duplicates.

M

Manifest

A module's mod.json, which names the module and says what it needs. See The module manifest.

Media service

The container, ems, that holds every media tool and model EiN uses: it reads metadata, makes thumbnails and video renditions, finds faces, voices and text, transcribes speech and makes the embeddings search compares. It keeps no data of its own. The Archive needs it. See The media service.

Mode

The permission bits of a node: read, write and execute, once for the owner, once for the group and once for everyone else, written as 0750 or rwxr-x---. chmod changes it. See Permissions in depth.

Module

A folder with a manifest that adds to EiN what its folders hold: commands, services, pipelines, pages and sidebar entries, settings, node types and documentation. The published modules are archive, assistant and ems. An administrator installs them on Settings → Modules or with mod install. See Modules.

Mount

A folder of another machine, reached over SFTP, shown at a path in the tree. An administrator makes one with mount. See Reaching other machines.

N

News

The timeline on Home of the albums people made lately. See Home, memories and statistics.

Node

Anything EiN keeps: a photo, an album, a person, an account, a log, a command. Every node has a type, a name in its folder, an owner, a group, a mode and the attributes its type needs, and any node can have children. See Nodes and node types.

Node type

What decides what a node holds and how EiN treats it, such as file, album, person or log. Core has types of its own and modules add theirs. See Node types.

O

On this day

The row on Home with a ring for each year that has pictures taken on today's date. A ring opens that day's memories story. See Home, memories and statistics.

Organizer

The part of the Archive that sorts the files in your staging area into groups, within a few minutes of their arrival. It leaves alone a group you have edited. The staging-organizer service runs it. See Sorting in staging.

Owner

The account a node belongs to, usually whoever created it. The owner's bits in the mode say what the owner may do. chown changes it. See Permissions in depth.

P

Path

Where a node is in the tree, from /, as a file has: /archive/albums/christmas-2019. See The tree.

Person

Someone in the archive, with names, dates and contact details, under /archive/people. Parents, children and partners are links between people, and the photos a person is in are found by their faces. See People and faces.

Personal group

The group every account made on the Users tab or with user-create gets, named like the account: the account helen has the group helen. See Accounts and groups.

Pipeline

A definition of the change it waits for and the steps, each a command, that it then runs on the node that changed. Modules bring pipelines; core brings none. See Changes and pipelines and Processing and pipelines.

Pool

A node under /var/pools holding long-running jobs that wait for a worker with room for them. The Archive's media steps wait in /var/pools/media, where its workers cpu and models take them. pool stat shows what is waiting. See Processing and pipelines.

Position

Where a photo, a location or an event is on the map, kept as its child named position: a point, with a radius or an outline for a location. See Dates and places of pictures.

Q

Queue

A node that holds messages in order until they are read; reading one removes it. The pipeline engine takes its work from /var/queues/pipeline-engine. See Changes and pipelines.

R

Region

A box on a photo, kept under the photo's regions/: a face the detector found or a box someone drew. It says who is in it. In the viewer a face box no person is assigned to reads Unassigned. See People and faces.

Rendition

A version of a file that EiN makes for showing it: a thumbnail, a display size, or a video or recording the browser can play. Renditions are kept in files-volatile in the data folder and can be made again from the original. See Files and storage.

Router

A node that passes what is written to it to everyone reading at that moment and keeps nothing, such as /dev/events. See Node types.

S

Service

A long-running job inside the ein container, a shell script EiN runs as the administrator: the web server, the SSH server, the pipeline engine, the pool workers and tidying loops. Core brings nine, and modules add theirs. A service is not a container. See Services and logs.

Session

A signed-in connection, a browser, an SSH login or a service, kept under /var/sessions and removed when it ends. See Accounts and groups.

Setting

A named value that changes how EiN works, such as CFG_TRASH_RETENTION_DAYS. System settings, named CFG_…, are changed by an administrator on Settings → System; personal ones, named CFG_USER_…, by each person under Profile → Preferences. Both can be changed with config set. See Configuration and Settings.

Share link

An address that opens one album, and what it holds, to anyone who has it, without an account. It expires after 30 days unless you choose another time, and Delete link stops it at once. Share links are kept under /var/links. See Sharing.

Sharing

Who can see and change an album, a person, a location or a device, set in words with Share: Can view or Can edit for people and groups, and links for people without an account. It sets the same mode and access list as chmod and acl. See Sharing.

Shell

EiN's command language: bash syntax, run inside EiN, where the files and folders are the nodes of the tree. The terminal, SSH and the assistant all run it. See The shell language.

Side drawer

The panel at the right of the screen, where the chat opens. See Finding your way around.

Sidebar

The column of entries down the left of every page. Core gives Home, Search, Settings, Statistics and Documentation, and modules add theirs. See Your sidebar and top bar.

Softlink

A node that points at another node by its path; opening it opens what it points at. Album covers, pictures added to an album from elsewhere, labels, favourites and relations between people are softlinks. Moving a node re-points every softlink to it. See Links.

Stack

Similar photos taken in a row, folded behind one tile in an album's grid, with the count in its corner. No file moves, and a stack belongs to one album. See Albums.

Staging area

Your private inbox for uploads, /home/<username>/staging, where files wait, seen only by you, until you file them into an album or discard them. It comes with the Archive. See Sorting in staging.

Step

One command in a pipeline. The pipeline engine runs short steps itself and puts long ones as jobs in a pool. Its status is pending, queued, waiting, running, completed, failed or skipped. See Processing and pipelines.

T

Terminal

The panel that drops down over the page with a shell of your own in each tab. The top bar's terminal button or Alt+T opens it. See The terminal.

Top bar

The bar across the top of every page, with the finder, Create, the terminal button and the account menu. See Finding your way around.

Track

The route a device recorded: GPS points with times, kept as the device's child named track, from GPX files or sent live. See Devices and GPS tracks.

Trash

Where what you delete in the web interface waits, in .trash in your home folder, for 30 days unless the administrator set another number in CFG_TRASH_RETENTION_DAYS. rm and Delete on the Nodes page do not use it. See Your profile, favourites and trash.

Tree

The one hierarchy of nodes that holds everything in EiN, from /: /archive, /home, /etc, /var, /usr, /dev and /tmp. See The tree.

U

Unidentified person

A person recognition started for faces that match nobody named, with a placeholder name such as Unknown 4XK9QM, waiting for someone to say who it is. See People and faces.

V

Viewer

The full-screen view of one photo, video or recording, with the details panel beside it. See Viewing photos, videos and recordings.

Visibility chip

The coloured word under an album's title that says how visible it is: Public, Restricted or Private. An album everyone with an account can see has none. See Sharing.

Visitors may browse

The setting under Settings → Permissions, CFG_GUEST_BROWSING, that lets people who have not signed in see what is granted to the guest group. It is off unless set. See Guest.

W

Worker

A service that takes long jobs from a pool when it has room. The Archive brings two, both on /var/pools/media: cpu, for work done on this machine such as thumbnails and transcoding, and models, for work done by a model in the media service such as finding faces. See Processing and pipelines.
