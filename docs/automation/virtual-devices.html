Automation

Virtual devices

A virtual device is a device of the Automation module's virtual driver: its readings are worked out from other devices' readings, and its switches and settings hold whatever people and automations set them to. It is a device like any other under /dev, with its page, its history and its readings on the bus, and it is shared the same way. This page is for members of the devices group, who make devices.

Use one for:

  • A reading of the house, not of one sensor: the average temperature indoors, the warmest room, whether any window is open, how many lamps are on.
  • A switch or a setting with no hardware behind it: away, a guest mode, the temperature the heating aims for, which automations read and set.
  • Something to share in place of what it is made of: the house's average temperature shared with a guest, who sees no single room's sensor.

Make one

# in EiN's terminal, as a member of devices
device-create indoors --label Indoors --class sensor --driver virtual \
  --config 'read=temperature=avg hall-sensor/temperature kitchen-sensor/temperature; open=any front-door/contact back-door/contact' \
  --config 'set=away=bool; setpoint=number 5..30 0.5 °C'

This makes /dev/indoors with two readings, temperature (the average of the hall's and the kitchen's) and open (true while either door is), and two controls, away (on or off) and setpoint (5 to 30 in steps of 0.5 °C). New device on the Devices page makes one too: pick the virtual driver and fill in its readings and controls as above.

Readings: read

Each reading is name=<what> <device>/<endpoint> <device>/<endpoint> …, separated by ; or new lines. <what> is one of:

What The reading is
avg The average of the sources' numbers
min The lowest
max The highest
sum Their sum: the power a whole floor draws
count How many sources are on (true, open, a number other than 0)
any True while any source is on
all True while every source is on
last The newest source's reading: whichever of two thermometers spoke latest

A reading is worked out again whenever one of its sources changes, from the latest reading of each, and kept when it changes. A source that has not reported yet is left out until it does. An average, a lowest, a highest and a sum take the unit of their sources when they share one.

Controls: set

Each control is name=<kind>:

Kind It holds
bool On or off; toggle turns it over
number [min..max] [step] [unit] A number, such as number 5..30 0.5 °C
enum <option> <option> … One of the options, such as enum eco comfort off
text Any text
momentary Nothing: a press, which automations hear, as a button's

Switch one as any control, on the device's page or with echo on > /dev/indoors/away; it holds the value until it is set again. An automation reads it with a device node and sets it with a control node.

Change one

The config is the device's own, so changing it is changing its config attr; the driver sets the device up again, adding the readings and controls that are new and removing those that are gone, with their history:

# in EiN's terminal, as a member of devices
attrs -J 'config={"read": "temperature=avg hall-sensor/temperature kitchen-sensor/temperature bedroom-sensor/temperature", "set": "away=bool"}' /dev/indoors

Who sees what

A virtual device is the devices group's, as every device is, and is shared on its page or with share-set (Share a device).

Its readings are worked out with the rights of the devices group and nobody's more. A source the group may not read, such as a motion sensor someone has narrowed to the parents, is refused, so a virtual device never shows anyone what its sources would not. A reading it works out is the group's to read, not to write: nobody adds a reading of their own to it.

When one does not work

What you see What it means What to do
device-create says the devices group may not read hall-sensor/motion, or there is no such endpoint The source is misspelt, gone, or narrowed to some people Correct it with device-show, or leave it out
its readings go round in a circle: indoors → upstairs → indoors A virtual device reads itself, or two read each other Read the sources themselves
the reading temperature takes one of avg, min, … <what> is misspelt or missing Use one from Readings
The device is offline The automation-engine service, which runs the driver, is stopped, or its config stopped working service start automation-engine, as the administrator; service logs automation-engine says why
A reading stays empty None of its sources has reported since the device was made It fills in at their next reading
