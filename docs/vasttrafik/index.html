Västtrafik

Västtrafik

The Västtrafik module shows public transport in and around Gothenburg on a dashboard and on the maps: when the next tram, bus or boat leaves a stop, where the vehicles are right now, and what disrupts them: a moved platform, a diversion, a line not running. It asks Västtrafik's open API for all three, with a key of your own, and a screen in the hall can show it as well as a phone.

Install it

Install it from Settings → Modules, or in EiN's terminal:

# in EiN's terminal, as the administrator
mod install vasttrafik

Modules covers installing and removing modules.

Give it a key

Västtrafik answers only an application it knows. Make one, once:

  1. Sign in at developer.vasttrafik.se and create an application.

  2. Subscribe the application to Planera Resa v4, for departures and vehicles, Geografi v3, for the list of stops, and Störning v1, for disruptions.

  3. Copy the application's authentication key: the key and secret written together as one string.

  4. In EiN, open Settings → System → Västtrafik, paste it into Key and save. Under Now the page asks Västtrafik whether it takes the key and whether the application subscribes to each of the three APIs; Check again asks anew. In EiN's terminal, the same is:

    # in EiN's terminal, as the administrator
config set CFG_VASTTRAFIK_KEY '<the authentication key>'
vt-status
# ok   Västtrafik at ext-api.vasttrafik.se takes the key.
# ok   Planera Resa v4 answers: departures, vehicles and their tracks.
# ok   Geografi v3 answers: the stops.
# ok   Störning v1 answers: disruptions.

The key is a secret: no shell sees it, the Settings page says only whether it is set, and EiN sends it to Västtrafik's address alone. Without it, the cards say that the key is missing; with an application that lacks a subscription, they name the API it lacks.

On a dashboard

The module offers two cards to Dashboards; the vehicles are a map layer, which the dashboard's Map card draws:

Card What it shows
Departures The next departures from one stop: each line's badge in its own colours, where it is going, its platform, and the minutes until it leaves as Västtrafik now expects it. A cancelled one is struck out in red, and a line Västtrafik reports a disruption on has a warning mark.
Disruptions What Västtrafik says disrupts some stops, and some lines anywhere along them: each with its lines' badges, its title, what Västtrafik says further, and until when it lasts. The worst first, marked red, then orange, then yellow.

Their settings:

  • Stop (Stops on Disruptions): type part of its name, such as brunns, and pick it. The list holds every one of Västtrafik's stops, over eleven thousand, and shows those that match what you type.
  • Lines: only these lines, chosen among those that leave the stop within the next twelve hours. Every line when you choose none.
  • Platforms, on Departures: only from these, such as A; a platform lettered A takes A1 and A2 as well.
  • At most, on Departures: how many departures it lists, up to 20. A small tile shows the first that fit.
  • Show, on Departures: the minutes until each leaves, or the time it leaves. With the time, a departure that is late or early has the time it was planned for struck out beside it.
  • Lines, on Disruptions: also these lines' disruptions anywhere along them, not only those at the stops. A line here is the one that calls at your stops: line 6 in another town is another line.
  • Also those starting within, on Disruptions: hours ahead, up to a week, to list a closure that has not begun yet as well; it says when it starts. None unless you set it.
  • What Västtrafik says further, on Disruptions: the description below each title, such as the reason and the hours it applies. On unless you turn it off. On Departures, a warning mark before a departure's direction says that Västtrafik reports a disruption on its line, wherever along the line it is; one after the stop's name, a disruption at the stop itself, such as a platform moved. Hover over the mark to read what it is; the Disruptions card gives the whole text.

The departures are asked for again as soon as EiN's answer is due, 20 seconds old unless set otherwise, and the minutes count down in between.

On a map

Västtrafik's vehicles is a layer any map in EiN can draw: turn it on under Map style and layers on the Map page, where it starts off, or choose it among the layers of a dashboard's Map card. The trams, buses and boats are drawn where they are now: each a dot under its line's badge in its own colours, as on Departures, driving along its line's track to the next place, with a short trail behind it. The vehicles are those of the whole map and a little beyond its edges, so that a vehicle drives in from the edge rather than appearing on the map. A map zoomed out further than a city's centre asks for none, and the layer's switch says "Zoom in to see the vehicles."; Västtrafik gives at most 200 vehicles at a time, which a large map of the city centre can hold.

On a Map card the layer takes settings of its own:

  • Stops: type part of a stop's name and pick it. Each stop is a dot under a white tag with its name, short, under the vehicles, and a card that has no Where it looks of its own shows the area around them.
  • Lines: only these lines' vehicles; every line when you choose none.
  • Metres around the stops: how much of the map that area takes, as how far it reaches beyond the stops, from 100 to 5,000 metres.
  • Trails: behind each vehicle, the stretch of its line's track it drove in the last minute, in the darker of its line's colours, and ahead of it, dimmed, the next 200 metres it will drive; both fade out away from the vehicle, which draws its trail as it drives. On unless you turn it off.

The vehicles are asked for again as soon as EiN's answer is due, five seconds old unless set otherwise, and each vehicle drives from where it is drawn to where the answer puts it, along its line's track and round its curves, or straight when it is off the track; it takes a second longer than the time between answers, so it never stops to wait for the next. Västtrafik moves its vehicles on between their own position reports, and when a report comes a vehicle may be put 50 to 200 metres back: on the map it drives on to where it was going and waits there instead, until it has passed that spot, so it never drives backwards. That needs its line's track, as Västtrafik gives the journey of a vehicle on it; the tracks are asked for every minute, so a line coming onto the map has one within a minute.

How often it asks Västtrafik

EiN asks Västtrafik only while a card, a map or a command wants an answer, and keeps each answer for a while, so that every screen showing the same stop costs a single call:

What Kept for
The list of stops a day
The lines at a stop 6 hours
A stop's departures 20 s, or Departures: 10 s–10 min
The disruptions 5 min, or Disruptions: 1 min–1 hour
The vehicles in a box 5 s, or Vehicles: 2 s–5 min
A line's track a day
Where a vehicle was 10 min

Each is kept from when Västtrafik was asked. A card asks again just as its answer is due, so the first screen to ask brings the new one and every other screen on the same stop has it at once: what a screen shows is never older than the time above.

The three that change by the minute are set under How often Västtrafik is asked on Settings → System → Västtrafik, in seconds, or with config set as CFG_VASTTRAFIK_DEPARTURES_SECONDS, CFG_VASTTRAFIK_POSITIONS_SECONDS and CFG_VASTTRAFIK_DISRUPTIONS_SECONDS. A change holds from the next answer, with no restart. Shorter is fresher and costs more calls: each stop on a screen is a call to Västtrafik this often, and each map one more, however many screens show them. Longer suits an application whose calls Västtrafik limits, or a screen that need not be up to the second; the vehicles then drive on along their tracks for longer between answers.

When Västtrafik does not answer, which it does now and then for vehicles, the last answer is given for a while longer: up to six times as long as it is kept, so two minutes for departures, 30 seconds for vehicles and half an hour for disruptions unless set otherwise.

In the terminal

The cards draw what six commands answer, which you can run yourself; a seventh, vt-status, checks the key:

# in EiN's terminal
vt-stops brunnsparken
# 9021014001760000  Brunnsparken, Göteborg
vt-departures 9021014001760000 --line 6 --limit 3
# 17:40  6     Kortedala  (C1)  +5 min
# 17:43  6     Länsmansgården  (B2)
# 17:45  6     Kortedala  (C1)  +2 min
vt-lines 9021014001760000
vt-positions --around 9021014001760000 --margin 500 --line 6
vt-positions --bbox=57.69,11.94,57.72,11.99
vt-tracks --around 9021014001760000 --margin 500 --line 2,3
# 2     Linnéplatsen  27 points
# 3     Marklandsgatan  34 points
vt-disruptions --stop 9021014001760000
# normal  2026-11-01 04:00 – 2026-12-20 23:59  lines 6
#   Linje 6, Rådmansgatan läge B är flyttad cirka 100 meter norrut.
#   Orsaken är ledningsarbete.

A stop is named by its gid, the 16 digits vt-stops lists. Västtrafik commands has every option.
