Recipes

Recipes

The Recipes module keeps the household's recipes: their ingredients and steps, how many they serve, how long they take, and pictures of the dish. A recipe's page scales the ingredients to the number of servings you want.

Install it

Install it from Settings → Modules, or in EiN's terminal:

# in EiN's terminal, as the administrator
mod install recipes

Recipes is then in the sidebar, and Recipe in the top bar's Create menu. It brings the Media module, which reads the recipes' pictures and makes their thumbnails through the media service; the Archive is not needed. Modules covers installing and removing modules.

Where recipes are

Every recipe is in /archive/recipes, one node of type recipe each, named after its title: Wrenfield apple cake is /archive/recipes/wrenfield-apple-cake. Everyone signed in can read the recipes, and everyone with an account can add and change them.

A recipe's pictures are files in its pictures/ folder, and one of them is its cover. They are not part of the library: /archive/recipes is marked unlisted, so the library's own views (the Media page, the map, On this day, search's pictures) leave them out, and it wants only renditions, so the pictures get their thumbnails and nothing else, also when the Archive is installed: no dates, faces, text or picture search. The recipes themselves are found by search, by their title and description.

Write a recipe

Choose Create → Recipe in the top bar, or New recipe on the Recipes page. Only Title is needed; fill in what you know:

  • Serves: how many servings the amounts are written for. A recipe's page can scale them only when this is said.

  • Time in minutes, Difficulty and Rating.

  • Categories: separated by commas, such as Baking, Dessert. The Recipes page narrows to one.

  • Ingredients and Steps: one per line. A line starting with # names a section, and the lines after it belong to it until the next such line:

    4 apples
# Batter
2 dl sugar
100 g butter

  • Language: the language the recipe is written in, such as sv for Swedish. EiN's own words stay in yours; a recipe's amounts are written the way its language writes numbers.

  • Taken from: the address of the page the recipe came from. Its page links back to it.

Choose Create. Edit on a recipe's page opens the same form; a field you empty is taken away.

Cook from a recipe

Open Recipes in the sidebar and pick a recipe. Type in Filter recipes… to narrow them by title, and use Category and Sort beside it.

  • The − and + beside Ingredients change the number of servings, and every amount EiN could read changes with them: 2 dl sugar for four becomes 3 dl sugar for six. A line without an amount, such as salt and pepper, stays as written.
  • Tap an ingredient or a step to strike it through, to keep your place while cooking. Neither the servings nor what is struck through is saved.
  • Upload in the page's header adds pictures, or drop them on the page. The first becomes the recipe's cover; under the recipe, the star on another makes it the cover instead.
  • Move to trash under ⋮ takes the recipe, with its pictures, into your trash.

From the terminal

The Recipes commands do the same from EiN's terminal: recipe-create, recipe-set, recipe-show, recipe-list, recipe-categories and recipe-cover.

recipe-create 'Wrenfield apple cake' --serves 8 --categories 'Baking, Dessert' \
  --ingredients $'4 apples\n2 dl sugar\n100 g butter' --steps $'Slice the apples.\nBake for 40 minutes.'
recipe-list --category Baking
recipe-show wrenfield-apple-cake
